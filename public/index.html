Process

Up and running in three straightforward steps.

No complicated Meta developer portals or business manager verifications. Just pair your device and set your rules.

01 / PAIR

Scan your QR code once

Link your existing WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business phone just like WhatsApp Web. Session credentials are encrypted into your database so the daemon reconnects automatically across restarts.

02 / MAP

Select your triggers

Choose where your messages come from: inbound document chats, a shared Google Sheet spreadsheet, or keyword requests like "ledger" from clients for Tally ERP.

03 / RUN

Let the daemon dispatch

The background process listens continuously, renders document previews, queries ERP data when requested, and dispatches messages at natural pacing without manual intervention.
